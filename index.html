🧪 Release Notes / Future Improvements

v0.6.6 — Clarity & UX Patch

This update focuses on first-time usability and removes common confusion points without changing the core gameplay loop.

What changed:
• Added clearer “No Active Dispatch” guidance for new users
• Added stronger Target Time / EFB Timer wording
• Improved insurance transparency in payout and company setup text
• Added aircraft/custom-flight reminder before dispatch
• Added Deadhead explanation for relocation costs
• Preserved the Clean Cockpit flow, Mini HUD, Pilot’s Choice, and honor-system design

Future Improvements (v0.7+):
• SimBrief launch support after outside-source testing
• Improved mobile input handling for ICAO and numeric fields
• Optional auto-distance estimation refinement
• Minor tablet/landscape layout tightening
• Expanded airport reference support
• More contract variety without adding heavy complexity

Built for console pilots and Free Flight users. Landscape is preferred on tablets/iPad, but portrait is fully supported. Future updates will continue to follow the KISS philosophy: lightweight, fast, and console-friendly.

🧭 Quick Start (How to Use)

Think of this as your EFB career overlay — not a mod.
Works alongside MSFS 2024/2020 or X-Plane using Free Flight.

1. Generate a Contract
Use "Quick Contract" or "5-Job Board" to pick a mission

2. Review Dispatch
Check route, distance, timer, and estimated net payout

3. Select Aircraft
Pick from your fleet (highlighted row = active aircraft)

4. Set EFB Timer
In-sim, set the timer shown in the Dispatch Card before takeoff

5. Fly the Mission
Use Free Flight — follow the route and complete the objective

6. Apply Results
Select bonuses/penalties honestly (pilot-driven system)

7. Complete Contract
Log payout, costs, maintenance, and progress your company

Note: Your fleet location follows your last completed flight.
Relocating aircraft may incur Deadhead costs.
